Selected projects

代表项目

  • Spatial immune barriers limiting CAR-T therapy in solid tumours
    限制 CAR-T 疗效的实体瘤空间免疫屏障
    Sep 2022 – Jun 2026 · Zhejiang University

    Built an end-to-end spatial and single-cell framework across patient samples and mouse models to explain CAR-T failure in solid tumours. Identified a CCR1-driven myeloid–fibroblast barrier; pharmacological inhibition dismantled it and restored efficacy. First-author manuscript under revision; patent filed.

    搭建横跨患者样本与小鼠模型的端到端空间+单细胞框架，解析 CAR-T 在实体瘤失效原因；鉴定 CCR1 驱动的髓系–纤维化屏障， 药理抑制可瓦解屏障并恢复疗效。第一作者论文修回中；已申请专利。

  • Epigenomics analysis of HDAC-inhibitor mechanism in CAR-T persistence
    HDAC 抑制剂提升 CAR-T 持久性的表观组学机制解析
    2023 – 2024 · Zhejiang University (collaboration with He Huang's group)

    Led the computational epigenomics analysis: chromatin (histone acetylation) and transcriptomic datasets defined HDAC1 → H3K27ac → Wnt/β-catenin as the axis behind improved persistence. Co-first author, Cell Reports 2024; reported at ASH 2023.

    负责表观组学计算分析：以染色质（组蛋白乙酰化）与转录组数据解析 HDAC1 → H3K27ac → Wnt/β-catenin 轴， 阐明持久性提升机制。共同第一作者，Cell Reports 2024；ASH 2023 报告。

  • Multi-omics dissection of liver cancer & transplant immunity
    肝癌与移植免疫的多组学解析
    Jul 2020 – Jun 2022 · Hangzhou First People's Hospital

    Molecular subtyping, immune-microenvironment profiling and candidate-target discovery on clinical cohorts; reproducible R/Python pipelines for clinical datasets. Outputs: Hepatology 2023, Redox Biology 2022 (both co-first), Cell Proliferation 2021.

    临床队列的分子分型、免疫微环境刻画与候选靶点发现；为临床数据交付可复现 R/Python 流程。 成果：Hepatology 2023、Redox Biology 2022（均为共一）、Cell Proliferation 2021。

  • Computer-aided drug discovery & molecular simulation
    计算机辅助药物发现与分子模拟
    2023 – 2025 · collaborations

    Docking, MD simulation and virtual screening for therapeutic-polymer and antibody-guided delivery programmes: Biomaterials 2025 (co-first), Nature Cancer 2026 (co-author). Tools: AutoDock, Vina-GPU, HDOCK, PyMOL.

    为治疗性聚合物与抗体引导递送课题提供对接、动力学模拟与虚拟筛选：Biomaterials 2025（共一）、 Nature Cancer 2026（共同作者）。工具：AutoDock、Vina-GPU、HDOCK、PyMOL。

  • Degradome-based plant miRNA–target networks & database (DPMIND)
    基于降解组的植物 miRNA–靶基因网络与数据库（DPMIND）
    Sep 2017 – Jun 2020 · Nanjing Agricultural University

    Built degradome-dependent miRNA regulatory networks in soybean and co-developed a public miRNA–target database and web platform. First author, BMC Genomics 2019; co-author, Bioinformatics 2018.

    构建大豆降解组依赖的 miRNA 调控网络，并共同开发公共 miRNA–靶基因数据库与网页平台。 第一作者 BMC Genomics 2019；共同作者 Bioinformatics 2018。
