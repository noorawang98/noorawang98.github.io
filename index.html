Research highlights

研究亮点

Spatial immune barrier in CAR-T resistance

CAR-T 耐药的空间免疫屏障

Integrated single-cell, spatial transcriptomic, multiplex imaging and clinical data across patient samples and mouse models; identified a CCR1-driven myeloid–fibroblast barrier excluding CAR-T cells. Pharmacological CCR1 inhibition restored infiltration and efficacy. First-author manuscript under revision at Cancer Cell; patent application filed.

整合单细胞、空间转录组、多重成像与临床数据（患者样本 + 小鼠模型），鉴定出 CCR1 驱动的髓系–纤维化屏障排斥 CAR-T 细胞；药理抑制 CCR1 可恢复浸润与疗效。第一作者论文在 Cancer Cell 修回中；已申请专利。

Epigenomics of HDAC inhibition in CAR-T persistence

HDAC 抑制剂提升 CAR-T 持久性的表观组学机制

Led computational epigenomics: HDAC1 downregulation → increased H3K27ac at Wnt-pathway loci → canonical β-catenin activation, countering exhaustion programmes. Co-first author, Cell Reports 2024; reported at ASH 2023.

负责表观组学分析：HDAC1 下调 → Wnt 通路位点 H3K27ac 升高 → 激活经典 β-catenin 信号并抑制耗竭程序。 共同第一作者，Cell Reports 2024；ASH 2023 报告。

Liver cancer & transplant immunology multi-omics

肝癌与移植免疫多组学

Molecular subtyping, immune-microenvironment profiling and candidate-target discovery on clinical cohorts; identified the SULT2B1-CS-DOCK2 exhaustion axis (Hepatology 2023, co-first) and CHI3L1 as a mediator linking sarcopenia to liver cancer (Redox Biology 2022, co-first).

临床队列的分子分型、免疫微环境刻画与候选靶点发现；鉴定 SULT2B1-CS-DOCK2 耗竭轴 （Hepatology 2023，共一）与连接肌少症与肝癌的 CHI3L1（Redox Biology 2022，共一）。

Targeted delivery & molecular simulation

靶向递送与分子模拟

Docking, MD simulation and virtual screening supporting therapeutic-polymer and antibody-guided delivery programmes: Biomaterials 2025 (co-first) and Nature Cancer 2026 (co-author).

分子对接、动力学模拟与虚拟筛选支持治疗性聚合物与抗体引导递送课题： Biomaterials 2025（共一）与 Nature Cancer 2026（共同作者）。
